Every block raises the same question: who is in charge of it? The answer sits right below the title, as small name badges with initials. That way you see it without expanding a block, in the editor as well as in the read view.

Every block can have responsible people, a break just as much as an input. Sections, breakouts and strands don't have their own; there you enter the people on the blocks.

## Add a person

1. Click **+ responsible** below a block's title.
2. Type the name. Below the field, the browser suggests the members of your workspace who aren't on the block yet.
3. Pick a suggestion or press Enter. Clicking outside the field also takes the name.

Repeat this for each additional person. Escape clears the field without adding anything.

## Members and external people

GoodWorkshop distinguishes two kinds of responsible people:

|         | **Member**                        | **external**                                                                 |
| ------- | --------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Who     | someone from your workspace       | everyone else, for example the client's management or an external co-trainer |
| Display | filled circle with initials       | dashed circle with initials and the word **external**                        |
| Name    | follows the member's current name | stays the way you typed it                                                   |

When you hover over a badge, it says **Member** or **Not a member of this workspace**.

**How GoodWorkshop decides:** If you type a member's name _exactly_ as it appears in the workspace, it becomes that member. Upper and lower case don't matter. Everything else is entered as an external person. A partial name like “Mira” isn't guessed, even if there is only one Mira. In that case, take the suggestion from the list.

:::tip
When a member changes their name in their profile, the editor automatically shows the new name on all blocks. For external people you have to update the name yourself: remove the badge and add it again.
:::

## Several people

A block can have several responsible people, up to 20. Each person appears only once on a block: a member who is already added disappears from the suggestions. If you type an external name a second time, it isn't added twice. The badges appear in the order in which you added them.

## Remove a person

- Click the small **×** on the badge.
- Or press Backspace in the empty **+ responsible** field: that removes the person added last.

## What happens to the names

- **Parking and moving:** The responsible people stay on the block, even when you park it or bring it over to another day. See [Parking area](/en/agenda/parking/).
- **Printing and exporting:** The names go along.
- **A member leaves the workspace:** The name stays on the block, as it was last saved.

:::note[For guests]
Guests who may edit an agenda don't get suggestions. Your workspace's member directory isn't meant for them. What they enter therefore always shows as **external**, even if it happens to be a member's name. Anyone who may only read sees the names entered but can't change anything.
:::

## Responsible people via an AI assistant

A connected AI assistant can also set responsible people. For a member, it needs their full name (first and last name) exactly as it appears in the workspace; any other name becomes an external person. See [Tool reference](/en/ai/tools/).
