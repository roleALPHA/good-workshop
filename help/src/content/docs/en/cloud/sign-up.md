In the cloud, everything starts with your company's own **Workspace**. The first 14 days are free
and need no payment details. After that, you decide whether to continue.

## Register

On goodworkshop.org, **Try it free** leads to the form, which you can also reach directly at
`goodworkshop.org/registrieren`.

1. Enter your **First name**, **Last name** and your **E-mail address**. You'll use this address
   to sign in later.
2. Enter your company's billing details: **Company name**, **Street and number**,
   **Postal code**, **City** and **Country**.
3. Enter the **VAT number** if you have one. For businesses in an EU country other than Austria
   it is required, because invoices are then issued without VAT (reverse charge). GoodWorkshop
   checks the number in the EU's VIES register.
4. Choose the **Billing model**: per user or per workshop. You can switch it every month later.
5. Check the boxes: that you're ordering as a business, that you're entitled to represent the
   company, that you accept the terms and have read the privacy policy, and that you enter into
   the data processing agreement.
6. Click **Register**.

The form offers the EU countries plus Switzerland, Liechtenstein, Norway and the United Kingdom.

:::caution[Businesses only]
GoodWorkshop Cloud is offered to businesses only. Without confirming that you're ordering as a
business, the form can't be submitted.
:::

## Confirm your registration

After you submit, it says “Check your inbox.” We send you a link; it's valid for 24 hours and can
be used once.

1. Open the e-mail “Confirm your GoodWorkshop registration”.
2. Click the link. The **Create workspace** page opens.
3. Click **Create workspace**.

That creates your workspace, signs you in and takes you to the library. You are the workspace's
first **Admin**. The e-mail “Welcome to GoodWorkshop” contains the contract terms that applied
when you registered – keep it.

| Message                                         | What to do                                              |
| ----------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------- |
| “This link has expired or was already used.”    | Just register again.                                    |
| “There already is an account for this address.” | Sign in with that address instead of registering again. |

If you already have an account and register anyway, instead of the link you get an e-mail
telling you that the address already exists.

## The trial

The trial lasts **14 days from confirmation**. During that time you can use everything without
restriction, and you can invite colleagues too. Nothing that happens during the trial is charged.

Below the header it says how many days are left, for example “Trial: 9 days left.” Under
**Billing** you see the exact day: **Trial until …**

If you haven't added a payment method yet, we remind you by e-mail at the billing e-mail address:
four days and one day before the end.

## After the trial

| You have …                 | Then …                                                                             |
| -------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| added a payment method     | everything continues without interruption. Billing starts at the end of the trial. |
| not added a payment method | the workspace becomes read-only: reading and exporting work, changing does not.    |

A read-only workspace isn't lost. As soon as an admin clicks **Add payment method** under
**Billing** and adds a card, everything continues.

:::tip
Add the payment method during the trial already. You're still only charged from its end.
:::
