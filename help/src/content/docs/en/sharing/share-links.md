With a share link you invite someone without a GoodWorkshop account to **one** workshop – a client, an external co-facilitator, the contact person at the conference hotel. The invitation goes to an e-mail address and only works together with that address.

:::note[Who may invite]
The workshop's owner and the workspace's admins may invite guests – the same people who may [share the workshop with people](/en/sharing/share-with-people/). Folders can't be shared by link.
:::

## Invite a guest

1. Open a day of the workshop and click **Access** at the top right.
2. Scroll to the **Guests without an account** section.
3. Enter the **E-mail address**.
4. Choose the **Permission**: **Read only** or **Read and write**.
5. Click **Invite**.

If [mail delivery](/en/account/mail/) is set up for your workspace, the guest gets the invitation by e-mail, and you see “Invitation sent to ….”

:::caution[Without mail delivery: copy the link now]
If no mail delivery is set up, GoodWorkshop shows the link directly below the form. Pass it on to the person yourself. It appears **this one time only** – only a checksum is stored. If you've lost it, simply invite the same address again.
:::

## What the guest experiences

1. The guest opens the link. The page shows only the workshop title: “Invitation to …”.
2. They enter the e-mail address the invitation was sent to and click **Open the agenda**.
3. They land on the first day of the agenda.

So a forwarded link, or one left lying around somewhere, opens nothing on its own. After too many failed attempts it says: “Too many attempts. Wait a minute and try again.”

### What a guest sees and can do

|                                                        | **Read only** | **Read and write**           |
| ------------------------------------------------------ | ------------- | ---------------------------- |
| View all days of this workshop                         | yes           | yes                          |
| See the workshop's [parking area](/en/agenda/parking/) | yes           | yes                          |
| Change the schedule (blocks, sections, duration …)     | no            | yes, live with everyone else |
| Add, delete, reorder days                              | no            | no                           |
| Rename, tag, move, delete the workshop                 | no            | no                           |
| Print and Markdown export                              | no            | no                           |
| Library, folders, other workshops                      | no            | no                           |
| Choose members of your workspace as responsible people | no            | no                           |

At the top, the guest sees “You are looking at this agenda as a guest.” or “… and can edit it.” Anyone who may only read also sees the **Read only** badge.

When a guest edits along, the others see them in [live editing](/en/agenda/live-editing/) under their e-mail address. They see [responsible people](/en/agenda/responsible/) who are already entered by name, but not your workspace's member list.

## How long a link is valid

The validity depends on the agenda itself, not on a fixed date:

- **The days have a date:** The link is valid until the end of the last dated day. If you reschedule the workshop, the validity moves with it.
- **No day has a date:** The link is valid until you withdraw it.

Both are also stated above the form, and in the list of guests each entry shows **valid until …** or **valid until withdrawn**.

## Keep track of guests and withdraw access

Below the form, every open invitation is listed with its address, permission, whether it has been opened yet (**opened on …** or **not opened yet**) and how long it is valid.

- **Withdraw access** ends the invitation immediately. An agenda the guest already has open stops working at their next step. The entry disappears from the list.
- **Invite again** – entering the same address once more – creates a new link and applies the newly chosen permission. The old link stops working. This is also how you change a guest's permission.

A guest who opens a withdrawn or expired link reads: “This invitation is no longer valid”. A new invitation from you helps them along.

:::tip[Shared devices]
With **End access**, a guest signs out on the current device, for example on the laptop in the workshop room. The invitation stays valid: the same link and the same address open the agenda again.
:::

## Share links through the AI assistant?

No. Inviting guests and managing links is deliberately only possible in the app, not through a [connected AI assistant](/en/ai/introduction/).
