:::

GoodWorkshop is available in two ways: you run it yourself on your own servers, or you use
**GoodWorkshop Cloud**. The software is the same. The cloud takes operation, updates and backups
off your hands – that's the difference.

## Who the cloud is for

GoodWorkshop Cloud is sold exclusively to businesses, to entrepreneurs within the meaning of
§ 1 of the Austrian Commercial Code (UGB). A contract with consumers is not possible. That's why,
when you register, you confirm that you're ordering for your company and are entitled to
represent it.

The cloud runs in the EU, operated by roleALPHA GmbH in Austria, with a data processing agreement
under Art. 28 GDPR.

## Cloud or self-hosted?

|                                  | GoodWorkshop Cloud                | Self-hosted    |
| -------------------------------- | --------------------------------- | -------------- |
| Cost                             | monthly, per user or per workshop | nothing        |
| Operation, updates, backups      | we take care of it                | you do it      |
| Trial                            | 14 days free, no payment details  | –              |
| Discover methods (**Discover**)  | yes                               | no             |
| Support form in the account menu | yes                               | no             |
| Where the data lives             | in the EU                         | on your server |

How to install it yourself is described under [Installation](/en/self-hosting/installation/).

## What only the cloud has

- **Billing.** Under **Administration** → **Billing**, as an admin you see the plan, usage,
  payment method, invoices and vouchers.
- **Discover.** A curated collection of ready-made workshop designs and building blocks that you
  adopt into your workshops in one step.
- **Support.** In the account menu, **Support** opens a form that creates a ticket directly with
  us.

Everything else – library, agenda, sharing, printing, export and the AI connection – works the
same in both editions.

## Your path through the cloud

1. **Register.** You create a workspace and try it free for 14 days.
   → [Sign-up and trial](/en/cloud/sign-up/)
2. **Add a payment method.** Before the trial ends, so you can keep working without a break.
   → [Pricing and billing](/en/cloud/pricing-and-billing/)
3. **Monthly billing.** Billing happens at the end of the month, and the invoice is under
   **Billing**. → [Invoices](/en/cloud/invoices/)
4. **Redeem a voucher**, if you have one. → [Vouchers](/en/cloud/vouchers/)

## Notices at the top of the page

If your workspace isn't simply active, a notice appears below the header, for example
“Trial: 5 days left.” or that the workspace is read-only. Everyone in the workspace sees it;
admins find the **Go to billing** link next to it. What the individual states mean is described
under [Pricing and billing](/en/cloud/pricing-and-billing/).

Announced price or contract changes appear there too, with the date from which they apply.
