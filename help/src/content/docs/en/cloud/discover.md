:::note
Discover is only available in GoodWorkshop Cloud. A self-hosted installation has no catalog.
:::

**Discover** is a collection of ready-made workshop designs and the building blocks they are made
of. You adopt an entry into your workshops whole or a piece at a time. The collection is curated
by us, not by the crowd: dozens of entries that we write and maintain ourselves, not a gallery of
templates for every occasion.

You'll find Discover in the header. All members of the workspace can browse it.

## What an entry is

There is only one kind of entry. A short building block with one day and a few blocks sits next to
a program spanning several days. Each entry in the list shows:

- how many days it has, for example **1 day** or **3 days**,
- how long it takes,
- how many people it is meant for, for example **8–20 people** or **any number of people**.

In Discover, methods are named neutrally after what they do, not after brand names.

## Search and filter

Above the list are the **Filters**:

| Filter                              | How it works                                                                                                                          |
| ----------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| the chips under each filter heading | Click a value to see only matching entries. A second click removes it again. If you choose several, an entry has to meet all of them. |
| **Group size**                      | How many people are in the room? Entries whose range covers that number appear.                                                       |
| **Time available, in minutes**      | Entries that fit into that time appear.                                                                                               |
| **Search**                          | A free search term.                                                                                                                   |

After entering a group size, time or search term, click **Apply**. **Clear filters** shows you
everything again. Further down, **Load more** loads the next entries.

:::tip
If you have 90 minutes, enter `90` as the time. You get everything that fits – whether a short
building block or a small program.
:::

If nothing fits, it says “Nothing matches that.” Then drop a filter or allow more time.

## View an entry

Click an entry. The page shows it day by day, with every block and its duration. Blocks that are
meant as a reserve in the entry are marked **Parked**. If a text isn't available in your language
yet, it says “This text is not available in your language yet.”, and you read the English
version.

**Back to Discover** takes you back to the list.

## Adopt into your workspace

Under **Adopt into your workspace** you choose where the entry goes:

| Destination                            | What happens                                                                                                            |
| -------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **As a new workshop**                  | A new workshop with the entry's name and all its days. It appears in your library.                                      |
| **Append to an existing workshop**     | The entry's days are added after the last day of the workshop you pick under **Choose a workshop**.                     |
| **Into a day of an existing workshop** | The blocks go into a day you pick under **Choose a workshop** and **Choose a day**. Only for entries with a single day. |

1. Choose the destination and, if needed, the workshop and day.
2. Click **Adopt**. The button shows **Adopting …**
3. Click **Open the workshop** to open the result.

What you adopt is a copy that you can change freely; the catalog itself stays as it is. After
that, the agenda calculates all times by itself as usual. You can only adopt into an existing
workshop if you may edit it.

:::caution
You can't adopt an entry with several days into a single day, or its day structure would be lost.
Use **As a new workshop** for that, or append it.
:::

## Notices after adopting

An entry never fails because of a single block. Whatever doesn't fit perfectly still arrives, and
the page tells you what:

| Notice                                         | Meaning                                                                                                 |
| ---------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| “… blocks arrived as notes …”                  | Your workspace doesn't have the block type. The block arrived as a note.                                |
| “… blocks arrived without their descriptions.” | Your workspace has customized the block type, and the text didn't fit into it.                          |
| “… days could not be written and are empty.”   | These days exist but have no blocks yet. Add them yourself, or adopt the entry again as a new workshop. |

## With the AI assistant

Your AI assistant can also search Discover and adopt entries, with the same three destinations.
The tools for that are in the [Tool reference](/en/ai/tools/).

Some entries can also be read publicly, on the
[Workshop methods](https://goodworkshop.org/workshop-methods) page.
