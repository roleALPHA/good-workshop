GoodWorkshop is a planner for workshop agendas. You add blocks with their durations, and
GoodWorkshop works out the clock times. Move a block, and the whole day recalculates. Anything
that must not move – lunch at 12:30 PM, the client meeting at 3:00 PM – you fix in place, and
everything else arranges itself around it.

## Who it is for

GoodWorkshop is for people who prepare and run workshops: facilitators, trainers, moderators,
internal teams. Everything you need in the room is right in the block's row: format, materials,
the people responsible and your facilitator notes, which only you can see.

![A workshop day in GoodWorkshop with times, durations and the people responsible](../../../../assets/en/agenda.png)

## What makes GoodWorkshop different

- **The agenda does the math.** Start times follow from the start of the day and the durations.
  The header shows how much content and how much break time the day has.
- **Cut without losing.** A block that no longer fits is parked instead of deleted – and can
  go back into the schedule on another day.
- **Made for the room.** The agenda works on a phone, survives bad Wi-Fi and lets your
  co-facilitator edit the same day at the same time.
- **No password.** You sign in with a passkey or a link by e-mail.
- **With your AI.** GoodWorkshop is itself an MCP server. Claude, ChatGPT or another assistant
  can create workshops and rework agendas.
- **Your data stays yours.** Every agenda can be exported as Markdown at any time.
- **Four languages.** German, English, French and Spanish. Everyone picks their own; what you
  write stays the way you wrote it.

## Two editions, one product

GoodWorkshop comes in two forms. Behind both is the same code: planning, sharing, printing,
export and AI access work the same way in each.

|               | Community Edition                                               | GoodWorkshop Cloud                                                        |
| ------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Operation     | on your own server, with Docker and a Postgres database         | by us, at [goodworkshop.org](https://goodworkshop.org), in the EU         |
| Cost          | free, including commercial use                                  | per active member or per workshop, 14-day free trial                      |
| For whom      | anyone who wants to run it themselves                           | businesses only                                                           |
| First sign-in | the first administrator, with the setup key from the server log | registration on the website                                               |
| Cloud only    | –                                                               | **Discover** (curated methods and designs), **Support** form, **Billing** |
