Everything that concerns only you is on one page: open the account menu at the top right and
choose **Profile & settings**. The page is called **Settings** and has three parts: your
profile, the language and, at the very bottom, **Delete account**. From there you also get to
**Security** (passkeys) and **AI Connection**.

## Enter your name

Under **Profile** are **First name** and **Last name**. This is how others see you in workshops,
in sharing and when you work live on a day together.

1. Enter your first and last name. Both fields are required, each with at most 100 characters.
2. Click **Save**. If everything worked, **Saved.** appears next to it.

If one of the two names is missing, the page reminds you: “Please add your first and last name so
others can recognise you.”

:::tip
The full name is also needed for [responsible people](/en/agenda/responsible/): a block can be
assigned to a member by their first and last name – even by an AI assistant.
:::

Admins can also correct a member's name, under [Members](/en/account/members/) with
**Edit name**.

## Choose the language

GoodWorkshop is available in German, English, French and Spanish. Each language appears in the
selection in its own spelling: **Deutsch**, **English**, **Français**, **Español**.

1. Under **Language**, choose the language you want.
2. Click **Apply**.

The language applies to three things:

| What                    | Example                                             |
| ----------------------- | --------------------------------------------------- |
| the interface           | menus, buttons, notices                             |
| your exports            | a workshop's [Markdown export](/en/sharing/export/) |
| the e-mails you receive | sign-in links and other messages to you             |

The choice is saved on your account and additionally in your browser. That's why the sign-in
page also stays in your language after you sign out. The sign-in page has the same selection –
handy if you're not signed in yet at all.

:::note
GoodWorkshop doesn't translate the content of your workshops. What you write in a block stays the
way you wrote it.
:::

## Delete your account

At the very bottom of the page is **Delete account**. This removes you from this workspace. If
your e-mail address belongs to no other workspace, your account itself is deleted too.

1. Click **Delete account**. A panel opens with the heading **Delete your account**.
2. The page tells you what belongs to you, for example “To hand over: 3 workshops and 1 folder.”
   If you own anything, choose under **Takes over** who gets it. Active colleagues are available
   to choose from.
3. Type your e-mail address to confirm.
4. Click **Delete account for good**.

:::danger
This can't be undone. Your access, tokens and shares disappear immediately, and you're signed
out.
:::

Two cases in which deleting doesn't work:

- **Nobody can take over.** If you own workshops or folders and there is no other active member,
  the page says: “There is nobody who could take over. Invite somebody first or delete your
  workshops.”
- **You are the last active admin.** A workspace never loses its last admin. Make someone else an
  admin first, otherwise nobody can reach the administration any more.

If, in the cloud, you want to end the whole workspace rather than just your account, that's
something else: it's described under [Pricing and billing](/en/cloud/pricing-and-billing/).
