A workspace belongs to a team. Anyone who works in it is a **Member**. As an admin, you manage
the list: open the account menu at the top right and choose **Members** under
**Administration**.

:::note
Only admins see the administration. As a member, the section is missing from your account menu.
:::

## Invite someone

1. Click **Invite a member**.
2. Enter **First name**, **Last name** and **E-mail address**. All three are required.
3. Choose the **Role**: **Member** or **Admin**.
4. Click **Invite**.

The person gets a sign-in link by e-mail and appears in the list as **Invited** until their
first sign-in. As soon as they open the link, they're **Active**.

If no [mail delivery](/en/account/mail/) is set up, the page shows the sign-in link directly.
Pass it on in person – whoever opens it is signed in with that address.

If the address was already a member, no second membership is created; the page then says
“… was already a member.”

:::tip
Someone who only needs to view an agenda or work on a single workshop doesn't need a
membership. For that there is [Share with people](/en/sharing/share-with-people/) and
[share links](/en/sharing/share-links/).
:::

## The two roles

| Role       | What it may do                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| ---------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Member** | Create their own workshops and folders and work with what is shared with them – reading or editing, depending on the share.                                                                        |
| **Admin**  | Everything a member may do, plus the **Administration**: members, branding, mail delivery and, in the cloud, billing. Admins can also open every workshop in the workspace; such access is logged. |

You change the role directly in the selection in the person's row in the list.

:::caution
A workspace never loses its last active admin. If you try to make the last admin a member,
disable or remove them, GoodWorkshop refuses: “Make somebody else an admin first — otherwise
nobody can reach the administration any more.”
:::

## Read the status

| Status       | Meaning                                                      |
| ------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Invited**  | Invited, but never signed in.                                |
| **Active**   | Can sign in and work.                                        |
| **Disabled** | Can no longer sign in. Name, address and workshops are kept. |

With **Edit name** you correct a member's name. Everyone should recognize what someone is called
in the agenda; that's why only admins may do this.

## Disable or remove?

Both take away someone's access, but with a different intent.

- **Disable** is temporary, for example during parental leave. The person stays in the list,
  and their workshops stay theirs. With **Allow again** they're back.
- **Remove** is final, for example when someone has left the company and wants to be deleted.

You can neither disable nor remove yourself. Ask another admin, or delete your account under
[Profile and language](/en/account/profile-and-language/).

## Remove a member

1. Click **Remove** in the row.
2. The page shows what belongs to the person, for example “To hand over: 2 workshops and no folders.”
3. If they own anything, choose under **Taken over by** who gets it.
4. Type their e-mail address to confirm.
5. Click **Remove for good**.

### What happens to their workshops

Nothing is lost, and nothing is quietly distributed. All of the person's workshops and folders
go to the person you chose under **Taken over by**. As long as you haven't chosen anyone, the
member can't be removed. A disabled member can't take anything over, because they couldn't sign
in.

:::danger
Removing can't be undone. The person's access, tokens and shares disappear immediately. If their
address belongs to no other workspace, their account is deleted as well.
:::

## In the cloud: who counts for the invoice

In the **Per user** billing model, only members with the status **Active** count, to the day.
Invited people who have never signed in and disabled members don't count, and neither do guests
without an account. More under [Pricing and billing](/en/cloud/pricing-and-billing/).
